Task and Progress Bar

First of all, we have our to-do list--used to keep track of your progress especially with your requirements because we know that you have a ton of them. Additionally, we have a progress bar--coded specifically to track your progress in those requirements. Go ahead: try it out!

  • Enter something in the task bar and click 'Add Task'.
  • The checkbox is used if you're done with that requirement. If you click it, the progress bar will be filled.
  • Theres an 'X' beside the task you have created. Click on it, and it will be deleted.
  • There is a '+' button beneath the progress if you want to play with it a bit.

Calendar

Next, we have our calendar which was created to ensure you don't miss any deadlines for your projects or requirements. We also have a whiteboard wherein you can type your notes

  • Double tap on the days of the calendar. You should get an alert, asking you to place an event.
  • Once you click "OK", you'll see your event placed on it.
  • There will be an 'X' mark beside the task. Click on it and it will be deleted.
